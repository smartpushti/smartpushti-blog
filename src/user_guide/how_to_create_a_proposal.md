## How to Create a Proposal (Earn AROGYA Incentives)

You can create a proposal in Smartpushti to **earn AROGYA token incentives** based on the work you do for the ecosystem.

Follow the steps below.

Navigate to Dashboard → Proposals → Create Proposal.

---



## Step 1: Prepare Your Document Link

Before submitting a proposal, you must create a **document link** that explains your work. Make the document URL short using a URL shortener.

Your thread should include:

* What you are building or contributing
* Clear description of the work
* Updates or progress (optional but recommended)


---

## Step 2: Stake 0.01 AROGYA Token

To submit a proposal, you must **stake  0.01 AROGYA token**.

This stake helps:

* Prevent spam proposals
* Ensure serious submissions

---

## Step 3: Submit the Proposal

When creating your proposal, you will need to provide:

* Document Link
* Your signature (already posted in the thread)
* Required stake (0.01 AROGYA token)

Once submitted, your proposal will go through evaluation. You will also receive a proposal ID.

---

## Step 5: Proposal Scoring & Rewards

Proposals are scored between **-5 to 5**.

### If Accepted (Score +1 to + 5)

You will receive:

* Your **0.01 AROGYA stake returned**
* Additional **AROGYA incentives** based on your score

Higher scores → Higher rewards.

---

### If Rejected (Score Below +1)

* Your stake may not be returned.
* You can improve your work and submit again.
